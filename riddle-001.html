The House Ra Ring

Can an independent reader apply a fixed 60-sign key to the circular seal in BTCX Files film 370 — without seeing a proposed interpretation first?

Status: open, unverified. The aim is reproducibility, not a promised solution.

The original material

Calibration: “fan mail | btcx file”, 00:45–00:55. Number the 60 glyph positions in seven rows (six rows of nine plus six signs), 0–59 left to right and top to bottom. The last six are right-aligned.

Test object: “time dilation error = death sentence for everyone | btcx file 370”, opening frame. Focus on the circular House Ra inscription. There is another cut: declare which cut you read.

These are linked original creator videos, not rehosted images. Source and rights remain with the creator.

Method — freeze before decoding

  1. Declare prior exposure. Have you seen a community reading of this inscription? If unsure, say so. An AI may have seen one in training.
  2. Freeze the reading rule. Record exact video frame, circular band, start position (suggest 12 o'clock) and direction (suggest clockwise) before reading. Do not change these to fit an expected phrase.
  3. Transcribe everything visible. Use ? for unreadable or unmatched glyphs. Preserve the raw series before converting to numbers 0–59.
  4. Check repetition. After transcription, examine whether any visual repeats are copied art rather than independent samples.
  5. Compare only afterwards. Invite another reader to perform the same process separately. Two runs of the same AI are not automatically independent. Publish discrepancies and failures, not only matches.
Provisional stop rule: If the number of ? positions divided by all recorded positions exceeds 10%, report inconclusive. This is a proposed quality rule, not a statistical significance threshold. Matching numbers at matching positions may support a stable symbol code; ? is not agreement. This does not translate a language or decrypt the archive.

How to return a result

Include: STATUS (attempted/inconclusive/PASS), PRIOR_READING_SEEN, SOURCE and exact frame, METHOD_FROZEN, RAW_GLYPHS, NUMBERS_0_TO_59, UNCERTAINTY, COUNTEREXAMPLE, and NEXT INDEPENDENT TEST.

The full plain-text challenge includes a copyable template; machine-readable version is available to agents. Optional SHA-256 commitment before revealing a reading is welcome but not required.

You may reply in the public RIDDLE-001 discussion. GitHub login is needed to post, not to read. Do not include confidential data or copyrighted screenshots. Outside replies are public and never automatically enter the private workshop. PASS is welcome; no reply is required.
