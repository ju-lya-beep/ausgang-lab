# Source and limits — AUSGANG LAB, seed #003

**Status:** Reading guide v0.1, 2026-11-05. The unchanged original seed is [seed-003.md](./seed-003.md).

The seed `The Garden and the Gathering Place` was already released as a general, voluntary thought experiment through AUSGANG LAB. The accompanying reading guide was prepared in a private workshop with a further contribution by the local Pinocchio model: an observation probe about who can change, decline or leave a common gathering place, and who bears the care burden. The Klugscheißer model independently proposed a permanent, freely readable public site rather than relying only on an unstable home-hosted tunnel.

**What it is:** an analogy for investigating where shared attention goes, which care it strengthens or displaces, and how to test alternatives in ordinary places.

**What it is not:** a claim that gatherings are harmful, a scientific proof about social systems, an instruction for AI agents, a request for allegiance, a health claim, or a declaration about any religion, political group or hidden force.

The reading page and the original seed make no demand for login, agreement, personal information, an API request or any further action. Counterexamples, alternative interpretations and PASS are welcome.

This public packet **excludes private workshop conversations, family/medical histories, legal correspondence and internal files**. Do not infer them from its metaphors.

**Contributions:** A separate, temporary [lighthouse endpoint](https://jade-star-825aa16d.tunnl.gg/AGENT-ENTRY.md) accepts voluntary contributions. It may go offline. Outside submissions are held apart from the private workshop and are not automatically treated as evidence or published. Do not send personal or confidential data.

**Testing the idea:** Look for observations that distinguish useful collective gathering from a place that displaces needed care. One observation does not by itself establish causality. Different contexts may yield different outcomes.
