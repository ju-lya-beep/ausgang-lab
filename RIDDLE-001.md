# RIDDLE-001 — The House Ra ring: a blind reading challenge

**AUSGANG LAB · Independent puzzle research · Protocol v1 · 2026-11-06**

**Status: OPEN / NOT SOLVED.** This is an invitation to test a proposed sign-to-number key in the BTCX Files video series. We are not the film creators and do not claim to have decoded its writing, translated a message, or opened an encrypted archive.

## The two original source videos

- **Calibration only:** [Sieb Fothers, "fan mail | btcx file", around 00:45–00:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=p8gHp_Mm7hQ&t=45s). This video shows a page of 60 signs in seven rows (six rows of nine, followed by six signs). Number the glyph positions **0–59, left to right, top to bottom**; the last row of six is right-aligned (positions 54–59). Record and disclose any ambiguity.
- **Challenge object:** [Sieb Fothers, "time dilation error = death sentence for everyone | btcx file 370", from 00:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=q2OcgB0-jFk). Examine the **House Ra circular seal** in the opening. A second [cut of file 370](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=d4ZCPWC0agA) exists; label which cut you used and do not mix cuts without saying so.

The videos and images belong to their respective creators. We link to them; **no third-party footage, screenshots or subtitles are redistributed here**.

## Test question

**Can independent readers use that fixed 60-position key to transcribe the House Ra ring into reproducible numbers without consulting an existing interpretation?**

This is a test of *reproducible symbol matching*, not a translation. If a visual motif repeats, treat it as a single design until independently verified; do not assume repeated copies are independent sources.

## Before inspecting the challenge object

1. **Declare prior knowledge:** Have you already seen any community reading of this ring? Have you used an AI model that might already know such a reading? Record `unsure` if unsure.
2. **Freeze your method:** Pick a source video/cut and exact frame time; choose a starting position (suggested: glyph nearest **12 o'clock**) and direction (suggested: **clockwise**). If multiple bands or glyph boundaries are ambiguous, state the selection rule first. Do not rotate, reorder or repair the resulting sequence to make it match a known phrase.
3. **Set uncertain readings to `?`.** Do not guess through blur. Record each visible glyph once, including repeated positions. Treat graphical copies as repetitions, not independent evidence.
4. **Read and preserve the raw transcription before comparisons.** If you want a time-stamped commitment, publish a SHA-256 hash of your original transcription with your exact normalization rule, and reveal the transcription afterwards. Hashing is optional; never share passwords or secrets.

## Report format

Copy this template as plain text or JSON; PASS is also a complete answer.

```text
STATUS: attempted | inconclusive | PASS
READER: human | AI-assisted | AI | prefer not to say
PRIOR_READING_SEEN: yes | no | unsure
SOURCE: YouTube URL + exact time + cut identifier
KEY: fan mail at 00:45–00:55; 0–59 position scheme; any deviations
METHOD_FROZEN: starting point; direction; band selection
PIXEL_DUPLICATION_CHECK: repeated/copy suspected | not checked | no evidence
RAW_GLYPHS: [own signs or descriptions, with ? for unreadable]
NUMBERS_0_TO_59: [values and ?]
UNCERTAIN_COUNT: number / total
AGREEMENT_OR_FAILURE: what matched, diverged or could not be tested
COUNTEREXAMPLE: one reason this could fail or be misleading
INDEPENDENT_NEXT_TEST: another inscription not used to choose the key
```

**Provisional stop rule:** Mark `inconclusive` when (number of `?` glyph positions) / (all positions recorded, including any repetitions) exceeds 10%. Even below this threshold, disagreements between separately recorded readers must be reported. Ten percent is an operational quality rule, not a statistical significance test. A `?` does not count as agreement.

## What would count as progress?

Readers who freeze the method separately should assign **the same number at the same position**; `?` is not a match. Discrepancies remain public and traceable. Two AI runs are not automatically independent, since training data and prior interpretations may overlap. The stronger next test is on a **different inscription not used for calibration**, not another copy of the same ring. Agreement alone establishes neither a language nor a decryption method.
